Fehrest-e kamel-e dastoor-haye GameShell
=======================================

In fehrest-e dastoor-ha hamchonin chon yek safhe-ye dasturi-ye UNIX mojood bashe:

  man gsh


Dastoor-haye bazikon
--------------------

gsh check
  barresi kardan ke aya hadaf-e mohem-e alan anjam shode ya na

gsh exit
  kharej shodan az GameShell
  lotfan be nazar dashtin ke mitoonid ba Control-d ham az GameShell kharej shodin

gsh goal [N]
  neshoon dadan-e hadaf-e mohem-e alan
  agar N dade shavad, hadaf-e mohem-e N ro neshoon mide

gsh help
  peyam-e komak-e kootah

gsh reset
  bazsazi-e mohem-e alan


Dastoor-haye modir
------------------

gsh auto
  ejra kardan-e skrip-e khodkar-e mohem-e alan, dar soorat-e vojood

gsh goto [N]
  be soorat-e rast be mohem-e N berid
  (lotfan be nazar dashtin ke agar N yek mohem-e ghabel-e ghabele beshavad, niaz be ramz-e oboor nist)

gsh skip
  dast keshidan az mohem-e alan va raftan be badi
  (niaz be ramz-e oboor nist agar mohem ghabel-e ghabele beshavad)


Dastoor-haye ezaafi
--------------------

gsh index
  neshoon dadan-e fehrest-e mokhtlef-ha, ba vaziyat-e anha

gsh stat
  namayesh-e amar-e sade dar mored-e mokhtlef-haye anjam shode

gsh version
  neshoon dadan-e version-e GameShell

gsh welcome
  neshoon dadan-e peyam-e khosh-amadid


Dastoor-haye barnamehnoosan
---------------------------

gsh assert shart [MSG]
  barresi kardan ke aya shart durast hast ya na
  agar nabashad, peyam-e khata neshoon midah

gsh assert check true/false [MSG]
  barresi kardan ke aya hadaf-e mohem-e alan anjam shode
  agar natije ba natije-e mohtabar mosabeghat nadashte bashad, peyam-e khata neshoon midah

gsh env
  namayesh-e motaghayer-haye mohit-e dakheli

gsh hardreset
  bazsazi-e mohem-e alan ba shoru-e dobare-e shell

gsh HELP
  in peyam

gsh log [-v]
  neshoon dadan-e log file-e dastoor-haye gsh

gsh pcm
  chape kardan-e shomare-e mohem-e alan

gsh protect va gsh unprotect
  ezafe kardan/hazf kardan-e hemayat az poshtheh-haye GameShell

gsh resetstatic
  bazsaz-i dobare-e hameye qismat-haye sabet-e donya

gsh save FILENAME
  bazi-e alan ra be file negah dashtan

gsh systemconfig
  namayesh-e tanzimat-e sistem

gsh test
  ejra kardan-e skrip-e azmoo-n-e mohem-e alan, dar soorat-e vojood
