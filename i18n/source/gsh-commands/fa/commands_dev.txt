**gsh assert condition** [__MSG__]::
	bokonin ke shart khahin ya na +
	agar nabashe, peygham-e khata neshan midahad

**gsh assert check true/false** [__MSG__]::
	bokonin ke hadaf-e mohemman-e jari takmil shode ya na +
	agar natije ba natije-e montazer mosaheghe nabashad, peygham-e khata
	neshan midahad

**gsh env**::
	neshan dadan motaghayyer-ha-ye mahali-ye mohit

**gsh hardreset**::
	bazneshani mohemman-e jari ba bash kardan mohit-e shell

**gsh HELP**::
	neshan dadan estefade-e pishrafte, ba hame-ye dastoor-ha-ye GameShell

**gsh log** [**-v**]::
	neshan dadan dosie-ye log-e dastoor-ha-ye gsh

**gsh pcm**::
	chap kardan shomare-ye mohemman-e jari

**gsh protect** va **gsh unprotect**::
	afzoodan / bardashtan hefazat az payeha-ye GameShell

**gsh resetstatic**::
	baz sazish hame-ye bargzide-ha-ye sarshahr-e donya

**gsh save** __FILENAME__::
	zakhire kardan bazi-e jari dar dosie

**gsh systemconfig**::
	neshan dadan tamim-e peyvand-e sistem

**gsh test**::
	efad-e script-e azmoony-e mohemman-e jari, agar vojood dashte bashad
