**gsh check**::
	check bokonin ke hadaf-e mohemman-e jari be anjam reside ya na

**gsh exit**::
	khoroj az GameShell +
	bedunid ke shoma mitoonid ba Control-d ham az GameShell khoroj beshid

**gsh goal** [__N__]::
	neshan dadan hadaf-e mohemman-e jari +
	agar __N__ vared shod, hadaf-e mohemman-e __N__ neshan dade mishavad

**gsh help**::
	payam-e komaki kootah

**gsh reset**::
	bazneshani mohemman-e jari
