**gsh auto**::
	efad-e script-e khodkar-e mohemman-e jari, agar vojood dashte bashad

**gsh goto** [__N__]::
	ravandan be mohemman-e __N__ bishtar +
	(tavajjoh: age __N__ yek mohemman-e ghabli bashe, ramz-e aboore lazem nist)

**gsh skip**::
	dast keshidan az mohemman-e jari va raftan be badan +
	(ramz-e aboor lazem nist age mohemman haghighatan yek bar takmil shode bashad)
