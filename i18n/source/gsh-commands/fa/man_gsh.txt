gsh(1)
======

NAME
----
gsh - farakhan-e dastoor-ha-ye makhsoos-e GameShell

SYNOPSIS
--------
**gsh** <__COMMAND__>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
GameShell yek bazi baraye yad giri (va amoozesh) estefade az shell-e
POSIX ast.

Taghriban hame-ye dastoor-ha-ye ke dar yek bazi moshahede mishavand,
dastoor-ha-ye adi-ye Unix hastand, ba istisna-ye dastoor-ha-ye ke baraye
tamass ba khode GameShell lazemand. Hame-ye in tamass ba **gsh** shoru
mishavad, ba dastor-e fe'li.

NOTE
----
Dar sistema-ha-ye adi-ye Unix, shoma bayad be tavaneed in dastoor-e
rahnama ba zadanne klid-e "q" khoroj beshid.

Shoma be tavaneid ba klid-e Space ya klid-e tereh harkat konid.

Shoma mitoonid dar in safhe jost-o-jo konid ba zadanne klid-e "/",
baad az an namune-i ke dar talashid. Ba zadanne "n" (baraye "next")
ya "p" (baraye "previous") be vaghe'e-ye ba'din ya ghabli-ye namone
jostojo miravid.

COMMANDS
--------
Dastoor-ha-ye adi-ye bazikon
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Dar halat-e adi, yek bazikon nabayad be bish az dastoor-ha-ye zir
nayaz dashte bashad.

INCLUDE fa/commands_standard.txt

Dastoor-ha-ye edari
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Chand dastoor hastand ke tanha vaghti bazikon ramz-e "modir-e GameShell"
ro midanad dastrash hastand. In dastoor-ha amalkardan be mohemman-ha
ro momkan mikonand.

Ramz-e aboor baraye GameShell-ye adi "gsh" ast vali agar shoma
GameShell ro dar kelase daneshgahi estefade mikonid, modarresetan
shayad an ra taghir dade bashad!

INCLUDE fa/commands_admin.txt

Dastoor-ha-ye ezafe
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Chand dastoor-e ezafe hastand ke etela'at dar mored-e bazi midahand.
Be joz command-e **index**, baraye bazikon chandand jaleb nistand.

INCLUDE fa/commands_misc.txt

Dastoor-ha-ye barnameh noosan va modiriyyat
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Dastoor-ha-ye zir tanha baraye barnameh noosan-ha-ye GameShell mofidand.
Bazikon-ha behtar ast az anha doori konand.

Reporting Bugs
--------------
Behtarin tarikh baraye gozaresh-e khata, baz kardan-e issue dar
github ast: https://github.com/phyver/GameShell/issues

Shoma mitoonid e-mail be soraghe <mailto:pierre.hyvernat@univ-smb.fr>
ba tozih moshkel-e khodeton ersal konid.

Lota fan dar nazar begirid:

* ta'rief-e moshkel
* versiyon-e GameShell-e ke estefade mikonid (baste be **gsh version**,
  ya gozine-e **-V**-e skript-e GameShell)
* khorooji-e dastoor-e **gsh env**
* har etela'at-e ezafe-ye marbut.
